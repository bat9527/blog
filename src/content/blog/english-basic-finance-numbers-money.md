# Basic Finance English: Numbers, Money, Rates, and Fees

> 如果前一篇《基础英语总手册》是在打日常底座，那这一篇就是把英语往金融场景里再推一步。它适合你在银行、支付、贷款、报价、预算、汇率、利率这些场景下先把最基础的话说清楚。

---

## 1. Big Numbers You Will Use All the Time

### 1.1 Common number units

| Number | English | 中文 |
|--------|---------|------|
| 100 | one hundred | 一百 |
| 1,000 | one thousand | 一千 |
| 10,000 | ten thousand | 一万 |
| 100,000 | one hundred thousand | 十万 |
| 1,000,000 | one million | 一百万 |
| 10,000,000 | ten million | 一千万 |
| 100,000,000 | one hundred million | 一亿 |
| 1,000,000,000 | one billion | 十亿 |

### 1.2 Useful examples

- `1,200` -> one thousand two hundred
- `15,000` -> fifteen thousand
- `250,000` -> two hundred and fifty thousand
- `1,500,000` -> one point five million
- `2,000,000` -> two million
- `10,000,000` -> ten million

### 1.3 Decimals

| Number | English |
|--------|---------|
| 0.5 | zero point five |
| 1.2 | one point two |
| 10.75 | ten point seven five |
| 99.99 | ninety-nine point nine nine |

Examples:

- The fee is `0.5%`.
- The price is `10.75 dollars`.
- The rate increased from `1.2` to `1.5`.

## 3. Percentages, Rates, and Basic Calculations

### 3.1 Percentage words

| English | 中文 |
|---------|------|
| percent | 百分之 |
| percentage | 百分比 |
| rate | 比率 |
| increase | 增长 |
| decrease | 下降 |
| higher | 更高 |
| lower | 更低 |

Examples:

- The fee is `1 percent`.
- The rate increased by `5 percent`.
- The price is `10 percent` lower.
- The default rate is higher this month.

### 3.2 Useful patterns

- by 5 percent
- from 10 percent to 12 percent
- higher than last month
- lower than expected
- a fixed rate
- a variable rate

### 3.3 Mini dialogue

> **A:** What is the service fee?  
> **A：** 服务费是多少？

> **B:** It is 1 percent of the transaction amount.  
> **B：** 是交易金额的 1%。

> **A:** Is that a fixed rate?  
> **A：** 这是固定费率吗？

> **B:** Yes, it is fixed.  
> **B：** 是的，是固定的。

## 4. Exchange Rate and Currency Basics

### 4.1 Core terms

| English | 中文 |
|---------|------|
| currency | 货币 |
| exchange rate | 汇率 |
| foreign exchange / FX | 外汇 |
| local currency | 本币 |
| US dollar / USD | 美元 |
| Tanzanian shilling / TZS | 坦桑尼亚先令 |
| Chinese yuan / CNY | 人民币 |
| convert | 换算 / 兑换 |

### 4.2 Useful sentences

- What is the exchange rate today?
- The exchange rate is 1 dollar to 2,500 shillings.
- We need to convert USD to TZS.
- The payment will be settled in local currency.
- Please show the amount in both USD and TZS.

### 4.3 Mini dialogue

> **A:** What currency do we use for settlement?  
> **A：** 我们结算用什么货币？

> **B:** We use Tanzanian shillings.  
> **B：** 我们用坦桑尼亚先令。

> **A:** Can we also quote in USD?  
> **A：** 我们也可以用美元报价吗？

> **B:** Yes, but settlement is in local currency.  
> **B：** 可以，但结算是本币。

## 11. How to Learn This Faster

这类金融英语最容易学的方法不是背孤立单词，而是把词放进固定模板里：

1. 先记数字和金额表达。
2. 再记费率、利率、汇率三个核心词。
3. 然后反复练 10 个高频句型。

你可以每天只练下面这几句：

- The total amount is 500 dollars.
- The service fee is 1 percent.
- The exchange rate is 1 to 2,500.
- The annual interest rate is 18 percent.
- The repayment is due next Monday.

只要这些句子你能顺口说出来，后面再去学更复杂的金融英语就会轻松很多。
